# Tab Refresh – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Tab Refresh does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data.

- **No data collection:** The extension does not read page content, browsing history, or any personal information.
- **Local only:** It keeps a short list of which tabs are refreshing and their delay. This list lives in Chrome's session storage on your device and is cleared when the browser closes.
- **No network requests:** The extension never sends data to any server.
- **No third parties:** There is no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
